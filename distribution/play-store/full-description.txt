BillZap is a GST billing app for Indian shops. Make a GST tax invoice in about 30 seconds, put a UPI QR code on it, and share the PDF on WhatsApp, even with no internet.

No sign-up. No subscription. No invoice limit. Your data stays on your phone.

★ GST INVOICES, DONE RIGHT
• Tax invoices with your GSTIN and your customer's GSTIN
• HSN and SAC codes on every line, saved once with each item
• CGST + SGST or IGST chosen automatically from the place of supply
• All GST rates: 0%, 0.25%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% and 40%
• GST rate suggested from the item name
• Month-wise GST summary for GSTR-1, exported as PDF or CSV for your CA

★ GET PAID FASTER
• UPI QR code on every unpaid bill: the customer scans with any UPI app and the money goes straight to your bank
• Share bills on WhatsApp with a UPI payment link
• Bank name, account number and IFSC printed on the invoice
• Outstanding bills sorted oldest first, with 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ day ageing

★ FAST BILLING AT THE COUNTER
• Saved customers and product catalogue for one-tap billing
• Voice billing: speak the item and amount in your own language
• Print on WiFi or Bluetooth printers
• Clean PDF invoices with a correct ₹ symbol
• Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts

★ REPORTS FOR YOUR SHOP
• Monthly revenue with a six-month chart
• Profit and loss, expenses by category and top customers
• Day close: cash in, UPI in, expenses out and the expected cash in the drawer
• Indian number format: ₹12,34,567

★ WORKS OFFLINE, STAYS PRIVATE
• 100% offline: billing, reports and storage need no internet
• No account, phone number, OTP or email
• No cloud server: your invoices stay on your phone
• App lock with PIN or fingerprint
• Backup to a file you keep, and restore on a new phone

★ IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Odia and Urdu.

★ MADE FOR
Kirana and general stores, medical shops, hardware, electronics, mobile and textile shops, wholesalers and distributors, and service providers who need GST bills with SAC codes.

Good to know: BillZap does not generate e-invoices (IRN) or e-way bills. It is built for small businesses billing at the counter.
