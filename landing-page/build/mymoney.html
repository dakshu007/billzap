Meet Riya. Second-year B.Com, Coimbatore.

Every month her parents send ₹6,000 for food, travel and everything else. Every month it runs out early. She never bought anything big. So where did it go?

Questions

Is My Money really free? {{ico:chevron-down}}

Yes. It is part of BillZap, which is free with no subscription and no ads in the app.

Do I need to run a business to use it? {{ico:chevron-down}}

No. BillZap asks for a business name during setup because its main job is billing for shops. Type your own name and use only My Money if you like.

How is "safe to spend today" worked out? {{ico:chevron-down}}

It takes what is left of your monthly budget, adds back what you've already spent today, and divides by the days left in the month, today included. Then it subtracts today's spending so far. Spend less and the number for the coming days goes up.

Can it read my bank SMS or UPI history? {{ico:chevron-down}}

No, and that is on purpose. You add expenses yourself, which takes seconds. My Money never reads your messages, contacts or bank accounts, and nothing leaves your phone.

Does it work on iPhone? {{ico:chevron-down}}

Not yet. BillZap is on Android for now.
