What is BillZap?

BillZap is a free GST billing and invoicing app for Android, made for small shops and traders in India. It makes GST-compliant tax invoices with HSN codes and an automatic CGST, SGST or IGST split, prints a UPI QR code so the customer can pay on the spot, and shares the bill as a PDF on WhatsApp. It works fully offline, needs no sign-up, keeps every record on the phone, and comes in 12 Indian languages.

GST, explained

What a GST invoice needs, in plain words

The rules BillZap follows for you, so you know what is on your bill and why. Reviewed {{updated_home}}.

What must a GST tax invoice include?

Under Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, 2017, a tax invoice shows:

  • Your name, address and GSTIN
  • A serial number of up to 16 characters, unique for the financial year, and the date of issue
  • The buyer's name and address, and their GSTIN if they are registered
  • The HSN code for goods or SAC code for services, with the description, quantity and unit
  • The taxable value, after any discount
  • The rate and amount of CGST and SGST, or of IGST, and of cess where it applies
  • The place of supply, with the state's name, when the sale is to another state
  • Whether tax is payable on reverse charge, and your signature or digital signature

CGST + SGST or IGST — which one applies?

It depends on where the supply takes place. When your shop and the place of supply are in the same state, the GST rate is split equally into CGST and SGST. When they are in different states, the whole rate is charged as IGST. In a union territory without its own legislature, UTGST takes the place of SGST.

GST on a taxable value of ₹3,740 at 18%
Sale of ₹3,740 at 18%Tax charged
Buyer in your stateCGST ₹336.60 + SGST ₹336.60
Buyer in another stateIGST ₹673.20

The total tax is the same either way; what changes is which government it goes to. BillZap picks the split from the place of supply and shows it on the bill.

How many digits of HSN code do I need?

From 1 April 2021 (Notification 78/2020 – Central Tax), it depends on your aggregate turnover in the previous financial year:

Previous year's turnoverHSN digits on invoices
Up to ₹5 crore4 digits on B2B invoices
More than ₹5 crore6 digits on every invoice

Services use SAC codes, which begin with 99. In BillZap you save the code once against the item and it prints on every bill.

Which GST rates apply in 2026?

Since 22 September 2025, most goods and services are taxed at 5% or 18%, with 40% for luxury and sin goods and 0% for exempt essentials. A few items keep special rates — gold, for example, is at 3%.

BillZap lets you set 0, 5, 12, 18, 28 or 40% on each item, so the current slabs are there and so are the older ones, for any bill that still needs them. The split into CGST and SGST, or IGST, is worked out from whichever rate you choose.
