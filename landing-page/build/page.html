{{ico:shield-check}} Compliance

GST handled properly, not approximately.

Getting the tax wrong means reissuing the bill. BillZap does the parts that are easy to get wrong, and shows its working.

{{ico:calculator}}

The right tax, automatically

Same state as your shop and it charges CGST + SGST. Different state and it switches to IGST. The invoice screen tells you which one it has chosen and why, so a wrong place of supply cannot slip past you.

{{ico:file-text}}

Ready for GSTR-1

A month-by-month summary of taxable value, CGST, SGST and IGST, exportable as PDF or CSV. It is supporting data for your filing, laid out the way your accountant reads it.

{{ico:package}}

HSN and SAC on every line

Save the code once against the item in your catalogue and it prints on every bill that includes it, with the GST rate suggested from the item name.

{{ico:printer}}

Print or share

Send the PDF on WhatsApp, or print to a WiFi or Bluetooth printer from the same screen. The rupee symbol renders correctly in both.
