Why BillZap exists

Three convictions that shape every screen.

Not a feature list. The principles we refuse to compromise — even when the easy path is to add a freemium tier and a sync requirement.

01

Free, forever.

No freemium tier. No "upgrade to unlock GST" prompt at invoice #11. If we ever charge for something new, everything you use today stays free. Your shop's GST compliance is not a subscription product.

02

Offline-first.

Your data lives on your phone, encrypted by your phone's OS. If our servers are slow, BillZap still works. If our servers don't exist (they don't, today), BillZap still works. Power-cut at 6:42 AM? Doesn't matter.

03

Vernacular-first.

Twelve Indian languages, with voice billing in every single one of them. Most competitors cover two — sometimes five if they get state backing. We treat Odia, Punjabi, Urdu and Marathi as first-class, not "coming soon."
