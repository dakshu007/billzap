What is BillZap?

BillZap is a free GST billing and invoicing app for Android, made for small shops and traders in India. It makes GST-compliant tax invoices with HSN codes and an automatic CGST, SGST or IGST split, prints a UPI QR code so the customer can pay on the spot, and shares the bill as a PDF on WhatsApp. It works fully offline, needs no sign-up, keeps every record on the phone, and comes in 12 Indian languages.

GST, explained

What a GST invoice needs, in plain words

The rules BillZap follows for you, so you know what is on your bill and why. Reviewed 16 October 2026.

What must a GST tax invoice include?

Under Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, 2017, a tax invoice shows:

  • Your name, address and GSTIN
  • A serial number of up to 16 characters, unique for the financial year, and the date of issue
  • The buyer's name and address, and their GSTIN if they are registered
  • The HSN code for goods or SAC code for services, with the description, quantity and unit
  • The taxable value, after any discount
  • The rate and amount of CGST and SGST, or of IGST, and of cess where it applies
  • The place of supply, with the state's name, when the sale is to another state
  • Whether tax is payable on reverse charge, and your signature or digital signature

CGST + SGST or IGST — which one applies?

It depends on where the supply takes place. When your shop and the place of supply are in the same state, the GST rate is split equally into CGST and SGST. When they are in different states, the whole rate is charged as IGST. In a union territory without its own legislature, UTGST takes the place of SGST.

GST on a taxable value of ₹3,740 at 18%
Sale of ₹3,740 at 18%Tax charged
Buyer in your stateCGST ₹336.60 + SGST ₹336.60
Buyer in another stateIGST ₹673.20

The total tax is the same either way; what changes is which government it goes to. BillZap picks the split from the place of supply and shows it on the bill.

How many digits of HSN code do I need?

From 1 April 2021 (Notification 78/2020 – Central Tax), it depends on your aggregate turnover in the previous financial year:

Previous year's turnoverHSN digits on invoices
Up to ₹5 crore4 digits on B2B invoices
More than ₹5 crore6 digits on every invoice

Services use SAC codes, which begin with 99. In BillZap you save the code once against the item and it prints on every bill.

Which GST rates apply in 2026?

Since 22 September 2025, most goods and services are taxed at 5% or 18%, with 40% for luxury and sin goods and 0% for exempt essentials. A few items keep special rates — gold, for example, is at 3%.

BillZap lets you set 0, 5, 12, 18, 28 or 40% on each item, so the current slabs are there and so are the older ones, for any bill that still needs them. The split into CGST and SGST, or IGST, is worked out from whichever rate you choose.

The GST terms on your bill

GSTIN
The 15-character GST Identification Number given to every registered business. The first two digits are the state code and the next ten are the business's PAN.
HSN code
Harmonised System of Nomenclature: the code that classifies goods for GST, printed on each line of the invoice.
SAC code
Services Accounting Code: the HSN equivalent for services. SAC codes begin with 99.
CGST and SGST
Central and State GST, charged together on a sale within one state, each at half the GST rate — 9% + 9% on an 18% item.
IGST
Integrated GST, charged at the full rate on a sale from one state to another, and on imports.
Place of supply
The state where a supply is treated as made. It decides whether the bill carries CGST + SGST or IGST.
GSTR-1
The return of outward supplies — your sales — that a regular GST taxpayer files monthly or quarterly.
Taxable value
The value GST is charged on: the price after any discount, before tax.

A plain-language summary to help you read your own invoices, not tax advice. For your own business, check with your CA.

Questions

The things people ask first

Is BillZap really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no trial that expires and no cap on how many invoices you can make. The app you install is the whole app.

Does it work without internet?

Completely. Every invoice, customer, product and expense is stored on the phone itself. You can bill all day with the network off and nothing is queued up waiting to sync.

Are the invoices GST compliant?

They carry your GSTIN, the buyer's GSTIN, HSN or SAC codes per line, the GST rate on each item, and the tax split shown separately as CGST and SGST or as IGST. Place of supply decides which split applies, and the app tells you which one it has picked before you save.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no phone number and no email. Open the app and start billing. Because there is no account there is also no server holding your books.

Can I send the bill on WhatsApp?

Yes — the invoice becomes a clean PDF with your shop's name on it, shared in two taps. If the bill is unpaid the message includes a UPI payment link as well.

How does the UPI QR work?

Put your UPI ID in settings once. Every unpaid invoice then carries a QR code the customer scans with any UPI app to pay you directly. The money goes straight to your bank; BillZap is not in the middle of the payment.

What is voice billing?

Speak the item and the amount in your own language and the invoice fills itself in, so your hands can stay on the goods. It understands all twelve of the languages the app is translated into.

Can my accountant get what they need?

Yes. Export a month-by-month GST summary laid out for GSTR-1, a profit and loss statement, a revenue report and an invoice status report — as PDF, or as CSV they can open in a spreadsheet.

What happens if I change phones?

Take a backup from settings — it is a single file you keep — and restore it on the new phone. Your invoices, customers, products and expenses come back.

Does BillZap follow the new GST rates from September 2025?

Yes. You set the rate on each item — 0, 5, 12, 18, 28 or 40% — so the current 5%, 18% and 40% slabs are there, and so are 12% and 28% for any bill that still needs them. The CGST and SGST, or IGST, split is worked out from the rate you choose.

How many digits of HSN code does the invoice show?

Whatever you save against the item — 4, 6 or 8 digits. Businesses with turnover up to ₹5 crore in the previous year need at least 4 digits on B2B invoices; above ₹5 crore, 6 digits on every invoice.

Can I print the bill?

Yes — print to a WiFi or Bluetooth printer from the invoice screen, or share the PDF and print it from any other device.

Is BillZap available on iPhone or a computer?

BillZap is an Android app, for Android 7.0 and above. The invoices it makes are ordinary PDFs, so your customers and your accountant can open them on any phone or computer.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (Nougat) and above. The download is about 32 MB — one file that installs on any Android phone, so there is nothing to choose between.
