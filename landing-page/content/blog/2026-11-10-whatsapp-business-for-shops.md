For most Indian shops, WhatsApp already is the order book. Customers send a voice note with the list, ask "bhaiya, rate kya hai?", share a photo of the product they want and ask for the bill. The problem is that all of it lands in the same personal WhatsApp as family groups and forwards, with no way to sort orders from chit-chat or see who still owes money.

The free **WhatsApp Business app** fixes most of that in an afternoon. This guide shows what to set up and in what order, with ready-to-use messages, a label system for orders and udhaar, the real limits on broadcasts, and the point at which a shop should consider the paid WhatsApp Business API, with Meta's current India rates.

## Quick answer

- **Use the free WhatsApp Business app**, not personal WhatsApp, for the shop's number. It adds a business profile, a catalogue, quick replies, greeting and away messages, labels and broadcast lists.
- **One number, one app.** A number can be on WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, not both at once. A dual-SIM phone can run both apps with two numbers.
- **Catalogue:** up to 500 products or services, each with a name, at least one photo, and optionally a price and a link.
- **Quick replies:** up to 50 saved answers, sent by typing "/". **Labels:** up to 20, for sorting chats such as "New order" or "Udhaar".
- **Broadcast lists:** up to 256 contacts per list, and only people who have **saved your number** receive the message.
- **API:** only needed for automation or sending to thousands. In India, Meta charges about **₹0.86 per marketing message** and **₹0.115 per utility message** (2026 rate card), plus your provider's fee.

For most shops doing a few hundred chats a month, the free Business app is enough.

## Step 1: Set up the account properly

**Pick the number.** Ideally use a separate shop number, so the shop's chats stay with the shop even if staff change. If you move your existing personal number to WhatsApp Business, your chat history moves with it, but the number stops working on personal WhatsApp. A landline can also be registered; it is verified by a voice call.

**Fill in the profile completely.** Customers see it when they tap your name, and an empty profile looks like a stranger's number.

| Profile field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | The name on your board, e.g. "Sharma General Store". Customers search chats by this name, so keep it stable. |
| Category | The closest match: Grocery Store, Pharmacy, Clothing Store, Electronics |
| Description | One line: what you sell, delivery area, "UPI accepted" |
| Address and map pin | Exact, with a landmark |
| Business hours | Real hours, including the weekly off |
| Website or link | Your Google Maps listing works well if you have no website |

Linking your WhatsApp number to your Google Business Profile helps customers find you both ways. Our guide to [listing your shop on Google Maps](https://billzap.app/blog/list-shop-on-google-maps) covers that side.

## Step 2: Build a catalogue customers can order from

The catalogue (Tools > Catalogue) is a mini shop window inside WhatsApp. Customers can browse it from your profile, add items to a cart and send you the cart as a message.

- **Limit:** up to 500 items, each with up to 10 photos or videos.
- **Required:** a unique name and at least one photo for every item. Price, description, a link and a product code are optional.
- **Review:** new items are checked against WhatsApp's commerce policy and appear once approved.
- **Collections:** group items, such as "Atta and rice", "Festive gift packs", "Phone covers".

**What to put in:** not all 3,000 SKUs. Add your top sellers, items people ask the rate for, combo packs and seasonal products. A medical store can list baby care and wellness items, never medicines, which WhatsApp's commerce policy bars.

**Share items in chat:** open any catalogue item and forward it, or paste the catalogue link in your Status and on your shop's poster with a QR code.

## Step 3: Quick replies for the questions you answer 40 times a day

Quick replies (Tools > Quick replies) store up to 50 messages, each with a shortcut. In a chat you type "/" and pick one.

| Shortcut | Message |
| --- | --- |
| /timing | Shop timings: 8 am to 10 pm, Monday to Saturday. Sunday 8 am to 1 pm. |
| /address | Sharma General Store, Shop 4, Main Road, near Hanuman Mandir. Map: [link] |
| /delivery | Free home delivery within 2 km on orders above ₹500. Orders before 6 pm delivered the same day. |
| /upi | Pay to our UPI ID sharmastore@upi or scan the QR at the counter. Please send a screenshot after paying. |
| /order | Thank you! Your order is noted. We will send the bill with the total in 15 minutes. |

Quick replies can also hold a photo, such as your price list or UPI QR. For the UPI side, see our guide to the [UPI QR code for shops](https://billzap.app/blog/upi-qr-code-for-shop).

## Step 4: Greeting and away messages

Both are under Tools > Messaging tools and go out automatically.

**Greeting message** goes to customers who message you for the first time or after a long gap. Keep it short and useful:

> Namaste! Welcome to Sharma General Store. Send your list as text, photo or voice note and we will reply with the total. Home delivery within 2 km. Timings: 8 am to 10 pm.

**Away message** goes out when you are closed. Set it to "Outside business hours" so it follows the hours in your profile:

> Thank you for your message. The shop is closed right now. We open at 8 am and will reply to your order first thing.

## Step 5: A label system for orders and udhaar

Labels (up to 20) let you colour-code chats and filter them. Most shops overdo it. Six labels cover almost everything:

| Label | Apply when | Remove when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New order | A customer sends a list or cart | You have packed it |
| Ready / Out for delivery | Packed or sent | Delivered |
| Payment pending | Delivered but not paid | Paid |
| Udhaar | Customer is on monthly credit | Account settled |
| Regular | Buys at least weekly | Never |
| Wholesale | Buys in bulk for resale | Never |

Each morning, filter by **Payment pending** and **Udhaar** and work through the list. The messages to send at each stage, from a friendly reminder to a final notice, are in our guide to [recovering udhaar from customers](https://billzap.app/blog/udhaar-management).

Labels sort chats, but they do not keep accounts. The actual amounts owed belong in your bills, where each one is marked paid or unpaid.

## Step 6: Broadcast lists without getting blocked

A broadcast list sends one message to many contacts at once, and each person receives it as a private message from you, not as a group message.

**The rules that matter:**

- **256 contacts per list.** For 700 customers, make three lists (for example by area or by type).
- **Only people who have saved your number receive it.** Everyone else silently gets nothing. Ask customers to save the shop's number at the counter; a printed card next to the UPI QR works.
- **Replies come to you privately**, which makes broadcasts good for taking orders.
- **Limits are tightening.** In 2025 WhatsApp said it would test monthly limits on broadcast messages and on messages sent to people who do not reply, to cut spam. The exact limits are not published and may differ by account, so do not rely on unlimited sending.

**What to broadcast, and how often:** once or twice a week at most. New stock, festive offers, a weekly fresh-arrival photo, a holiday closing notice. Never forwards, jokes or political content. If people block or report your number, WhatsApp can restrict it.

**A broadcast that works:**

> Diwali gift packs are in! Dry fruit boxes from ₹499, sweets hampers from ₹650. Reply with the pack number to book; delivery free within 2 km. Sharma General Store.

For more ideas that bring customers back, see our [marketing ideas for shops](https://billzap.app/blog/marketing-ideas-for-shop).

## Step 7: Status as a free shop window

Status updates are seen by people who have saved your number and disappear after 24 hours, which suits things that change daily: today's fruit and vegetable arrivals with prices, a short video of new stock, "back in stock" posts and festive timings. Post one to three a day and keep the price visible in the photo; it saves a round of "rate?" messages.

## When the WhatsApp Business API is worth it

The API (officially the WhatsApp Business Platform) is a different product. You cannot sign up directly in the app; you go through a Meta Business Partner (a provider such as a chatbot or CRM company) and pay per message.

**You need it if** you want to send automated order or delivery updates, message thousands of opted-in customers at once, run a chatbot, or let several staff answer from a shared dashboard. A single kirana or medical store rarely does.

**How Meta charges in India** (per delivered message, from Meta's INR rate card for 2026):

| Message category | What it is for | Meta's rate in India |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing | Offers, festive messages, new arrivals | ₹0.8631 |
| Utility | Order confirmations, bill and payment updates | ₹0.115 |
| Authentication | OTPs | ₹0.115 |
| Service | Your replies when a customer writes to you | First 1,000 a month free per number, then charged (from 1 October 2026) |
| Messages customers send you | | Free |

From **1 October 2026**, Meta also started charging for service replies beyond 1,000 a month per number and for utility messages sent inside the 24-hour customer service window, which were free earlier. Rates change every quarter or so, and your provider adds its own fee and GST, so check the current rate card before you budget.

**Worked example:** a shop sends 1,000 festive offer messages and 2,000 bill updates in a month through the API.

| Item | Working | Meta's charge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing | 1,000 × ₹0.8631 | ₹863.10 |
| Utility | 2,000 × ₹0.115 | ₹230.00 |
| **Total before provider fee and GST** | | **₹1,093.10** |

The same 1,000 offers sent through four free broadcast lists in the app cost nothing, but only reach customers who saved your number. That is the real trade-off: reach and automation versus cost.

## Meta Verified for business

Meta Verified is an optional paid subscription for business accounts. On WhatsApp it gives a verified badge on your profile, protection against people impersonating your business, priority customer support and extra features such as more linked devices. When Meta launched business plans in India in 2024, prices started at ₹639 a month for a single app. Plans and availability now vary by business, so check the current price in the app (Settings or Tools > Meta Verified).

It is worth it if fake accounts misuse your shop's name. A neighbourhood shop that customers already know rarely needs it.

## How to avoid getting your number banned

WhatsApp restricts or bans business numbers that look like spam. The safe habits:

1. **Message only people who gave you their number** for the shop, and stop when someone asks.
2. **Do not buy contact lists** or add people to groups without asking.
3. **Keep broadcasts relevant and infrequent.** Blocks and reports are the main trigger.
4. **Do not use modified apps** ("GB WhatsApp" and similar) or bulk-sending tools that automate the app. They break WhatsApp's terms.
5. **Do not sell prohibited items** through the catalogue, such as medicines, tobacco, alcohol or weapons, under WhatsApp's commerce policy.

## FAQ

### Is WhatsApp Business free for shops?

Yes. The WhatsApp Business app is free to download and use, including the catalogue, quick replies, labels and broadcast lists. You pay only for optional extras such as Meta Verified, or for the separate Business API if you choose it.

### What is the difference between WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business?

WhatsApp Business adds business tools: a profile with address and hours, a catalogue, quick replies, greeting and away messages, labels and more. Chats work the same way for customers. A number can be registered on only one of the two apps at a time.

### How many people can I send a broadcast to?

Up to 256 contacts per broadcast list, and you can make several lists. Only contacts who have saved your number receive the message. WhatsApp has been testing monthly limits on broadcasts, so avoid sending too often.

### How many products can I add to the WhatsApp catalogue?

Up to 500 products or services, each with up to 10 photos or videos. Every item needs a unique name and at least one image; the price is optional.

### How much does the WhatsApp Business API cost in India?

Meta charges per delivered message: about ₹0.86 for a marketing message and ₹0.115 for a utility or authentication message on its 2026 India rate card, with 1,000 free service replies a month per number. Providers add their own fee and GST.

### Can I use the same number on WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business?

Not at the same time. Moving a number to WhatsApp Business moves its chats too. To keep both, use two numbers, which a dual-SIM phone can run side by side.

## The bottom line

Set up the free WhatsApp Business app once: a complete profile, a catalogue of top sellers, quick replies, an away message, six labels and broadcast lists of customers who saved your number. Send offers sparingly and bills promptly. Most shops never need the paid API; those that do should budget from Meta's current rate card plus the provider's fee.
