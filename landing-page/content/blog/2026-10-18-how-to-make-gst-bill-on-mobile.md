## Quick answer: how to make a GST bill on mobile

1. **Install a GST billing app** (or open a template or online generator; the trade-offs are compared below).
2. **Enter your business details once:** name, address, GSTIN, state, and your UPI ID or bank details.
3. **Save your items** with their HSN code and GST rate, and your regular customers with their GSTIN and state.
4. **Create the bill:** pick the customer, add items and quantities, and check the place of supply so the tax is split correctly into CGST + SGST or IGST.
5. **Save and share:** send the PDF on WhatsApp or print it, and collect payment by UPI.

The first bill takes about ten minutes, almost all of it setup. After that, a bill takes under a minute.

## What you need before your first GST bill

Collect these before you start, so the first bill is not a scramble:

| Detail | Where it comes from | Why the bill needs it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business name and address | Your GST registration certificate | Must match your registration exactly |
| GSTIN | GST registration (15 characters) | Mandatory on every tax invoice |
| Your state and state code | First two digits of your GSTIN | Decides CGST + SGST or IGST |
| HSN or SAC code for each item | GST portal search, your supplier's bill, or your CA | At least 4 digits on B2B bills if turnover is up to ₹5 crore |
| GST rate for each item | CBIC rate notifications, or your CA | Since 22 September 2025, mostly 5% or 18% |
| Customer's name, GSTIN and state | The customer | Their GSTIN lets them claim input tax credit |
| UPI ID or bank account | Your bank or UPI app | So the customer can pay from the bill |

The HSN code is the one people skip, and it is the one that later causes trouble in GSTR-1. Our guide to the [GST invoice format](https://billzap.app/blog/gst-invoice-format) explains how many digits you need and what else Rule 46 requires on the bill.

## Do you need a GST bill, or a plain bill?

Not every business that bills customers should issue a GST tax invoice:

- **You are GST-registered under the regular scheme:** issue a tax invoice that charges GST. This is what the rest of this guide covers.
- **You are under the composition scheme:** you cannot charge GST to customers. Issue a **bill of supply** instead, stating that you are a composition taxable person not eligible to collect tax.
- **You sell only exempt goods** such as fresh vegetables or unpackaged grains: issue a bill of supply, not a tax invoice.
- **You are not registered for GST:** you cannot show or collect GST at all. A clean bill with your name, the items and the total is enough. A billing app is still useful for numbering, records and UPI collection; just turn GST off on the bill.

Charging GST without a valid registration, or charging it as a composition dealer, is a serious error. Settle which category you are in before your first bill.

## How to find the HSN code and GST rate for your items

If you do not know an item's code, there are three reliable routes:

1. **Your supplier's purchase bill.** For goods you resell, the HSN code on the invoice you received is usually the right one, and it is the fastest place to look.
2. **The GST portal's HSN/SAC search.** Search by product name or code on the official GST services site.
3. **Your chartered accountant,** especially for items that changed slab in September 2025 or that could fall under more than one heading.

Save each code once in your app's item list and you never have to look it up again.

## Three ways to make a GST bill on your phone

You can make a valid GST bill on a phone in three ways. They differ less in the bill they produce than in how much work the next 500 bills take.

| | Billing app | Online invoice generator | Excel or Word template |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Works offline** | Depends on the app | No | Yes |
| **Invoice numbering** | Automatic | Usually manual | Manual |
| **CGST/SGST vs IGST split** | Automatic in good apps | Usually manual | Formula you maintain |
| **Saved items and customers** | Yes | Sometimes, with an account | Copy and paste |
| **Share on WhatsApp** | Built in | Download, then share | Export to PDF, then share |
| **Monthly GST summary** | Yes, in most apps | Rarely | You build it |

**Templates** are fine if you make a few bills a month. On a small phone screen, though, editing a spreadsheet at the counter is slow, and the classic errors creep in: last month's file copied with the old invoice number, or the IGST formula overwritten.

**Online generators** produce a clean PDF but need a working internet connection for every bill, and you usually manage the numbering yourself. Your customers' details also sit on someone else's server.

**Billing apps** do the repetitive parts for you, including numbering, the tax split, the saved catalogue and the monthly summary. ## What your finished bill should contain

Whatever app or template you use, a correct GST tax invoice made on a phone carries:

- Your name, address and GSTIN, and the buyer's GSTIN if they are registered
- A unique invoice number and the date
- For each line: description, HSN or SAC code, quantity, unit and taxable value
- The GST rate and amount, split into CGST + SGST, or IGST
- The place of supply for inter-state sales, and whether reverse charge applies
- The total, plus optional payment details such as a UPI QR code

A bill sent as a PDF from your phone is an electronic invoice, and under Rule 46 an electronic invoice does not need a physical signature.

## Tips by type of business

**Kirana and retail counters.** Speed matters more than anything. Save your fastest-moving items first, use voice billing during rush hours, and keep the UPI QR on every bill, since most customers already have a UPI app open. For small sales where the customer does not want a bill, the rules allow one consolidated tax invoice at the end of the day, but most shops find individual bills easier to reconcile.

**Wholesalers and distributors.** Your buyers are mostly registered, so their GSTIN on the bill is non-negotiable; a wrong GSTIN blocks their credit and they will call you about it. Double-check the place of supply on every outstation order, and use the invoice ageing report to chase payments: BillZap sorts unpaid bills into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ day buckets.

**Service providers.** Use SAC codes, which begin with 99, instead of HSN codes. Issue the bill within 30 days of finishing the service. For a registered client in another state, the place of supply is the client's location, so the bill carries IGST.

## Mistakes that make a mobile GST bill invalid

- **No HSN code,** or too few digits on a B2B bill
- **One "GST 18%" line** instead of separate CGST and SGST amounts on an intra-state bill
- **IGST on an over-the-counter sale** just because the customer is from another state
- **Repeating an invoice number** after switching apps or reinstalling one without restoring a backup
- **A typo in the buyer's GSTIN,** which stops their input tax credit
- **An outdated GST rate,** such as 12% or 28% on items that moved slab in September 2025
- **No backup:** a phone is easy to lose, and the bills on it are records you must keep

On the last point, take regular backups. BillZap saves a backup file you keep wherever you like, such as Google Drive, email or a computer, and restores it on a new phone. It also has an app lock with PIN or fingerprint, so a borrowed phone does not mean borrowed books.

## When a phone app is not enough

A mobile billing app covers most small businesses, but two GST requirements need more:

- **E-invoicing.** If your aggregate turnover has crossed **₹5 crore** in any year since 2017-18, your B2B invoices must be registered on the Invoice Registration Portal for an IRN and QR code. You will need software that connects to the IRP.
- **E-way bills.** Moving goods worth more than **₹50,000** in a consignment generally needs an e-way bill, and some states set their own limits for movement within the state. Many shops below that value never need one; wholesalers usually do.

## FAQ: making a GST bill on mobile

### Can I make a GST bill on my mobile phone?

Yes. A GST tax invoice can be created and issued electronically from a phone, as long as it contains everything Rule 46 of the CGST Rules requires. An electronic invoice does not need a physical signature.

### Which app is best for making a GST bill on mobile for free?

It depends on what you need. For counter billing with no subscription and no internet dependence, BillZap is free with no paid tier and works fully offline. If you need e-invoicing, e-way bills or several devices, a paid app such as Vyapar or myBillBook may fit better.

### How do I make a GST bill without internet?

Use a billing app that stores your data on the phone instead of in the cloud. Offline-first apps let you create, save, print and share bills with no connection. Apps that sync to a server may stop working or queue bills when the network drops.

### Is a GST bill made on mobile valid?

Yes, provided it carries the mandatory particulars: both GSTINs where applicable, a unique invoice number, date, HSN or SAC codes, taxable value, and the correct CGST + SGST or IGST split. The method used to make the bill does not matter; its contents do.

### How do I send a GST bill to a customer on WhatsApp?

Save the bill as a PDF and share it from your billing app's share button, or from your files to WhatsApp. Billing apps such as BillZap do this in one tap, and can attach a UPI payment link to unpaid bills.

### Do I need a computer for GST billing?

No. For billing, a phone is enough. Your accountant may use a computer for filing returns, and you can send them your monthly GST summary as a PDF or CSV from the phone.
