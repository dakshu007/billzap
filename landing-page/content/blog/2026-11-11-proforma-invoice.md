A customer says, "Send me a proforma, I'll release the payment." A bank asks for a "proforma invoice of the machine" before sanctioning a loan. An importer abroad wants one before opening a letter of credit. In each case they want a document that looks like the final bill, with prices and taxes, before the sale has actually happened.

That is exactly what a proforma invoice is, and also why it causes confusion under GST. It looks like an invoice but has no legal standing as one. This guide explains what it is, when to use it, what to put on it, a sample with the GST estimate worked out, and how and when to replace it with a proper tax invoice.

## Quick answer

- A **proforma invoice** is a **preliminary bill** sent before supply, stating the goods or services, quantities, prices, taxes and terms the seller intends to charge. "Pro forma" is Latin for "as a matter of form".
- It is **not a tax invoice**. The CGST Act does not mention it. Issuing one creates **no GST liability**, it is **not reported in GSTR-1**, and the buyer **cannot claim input tax credit** on it.
- GST shown on a proforma is an **estimate** of what the final tax invoice will charge.
- **Common uses:** asking for an advance, a buyer's internal approval or purchase order, **bank loans and subsidies** for machinery, and **imports and exports** (letters of credit, advance remittance, import licences).
- **Advance received against a proforma:** for **goods**, a regular taxpayer pays no GST on the advance (Notification 66/2017-Central Tax). For **services**, GST is due on the advance, with a receipt voucher.
- When you actually supply, issue a **tax invoice** from your regular invoice series. The proforma number is not reused.

## What a proforma invoice means

A proforma invoice is a seller's commitment, in invoice form, to supply goods or services at stated prices and terms: closer to a formal quotation than to a bill.

Three things make it different from a real invoice:

1. **Nothing has been supplied yet.** No goods have left your shop and no service has been done.
2. **It is not a demand for a debt.** The buyer does not owe you the amount on it. It becomes payable only if the buyer accepts, usually by paying an advance or sending a purchase order.
3. **It can change.** Quantities, prices and even the tax rate can differ on the final invoice.

## Is a proforma invoice valid under GST?

Under GST, a taxable supply has to be billed on a **tax invoice** under Section 31 of the CGST Act, with the details listed in Rule 46. A registered person who is exempt from tax or is a composition dealer issues a **bill of supply** instead. A proforma invoice is neither of these. The Act and the Rules do not mention it at all.

That has four practical consequences:

| Question | Answer for a proforma invoice |
| --- | --- |
| Does it create GST liability for the seller? | No. Liability arises at the time of supply, not when a proforma is sent. |
| Is it reported in GSTR-1? | No. Only tax invoices, credit and debit notes, advances and similar items are. |
| Can the buyer claim input tax credit on it? | No. Section 16(2)(a) allows credit only against a tax invoice, debit note or other prescribed document. |
| Can goods move on it? | It should not be the document goods travel with. Goods move with a tax invoice, a bill of supply or a delivery challan, plus an e-way bill where required. |

You can put "GST" on a proforma, and usually should, so that the buyer knows the full amount. Just label it clearly as an estimate.

### What if the buyer pays an advance against the proforma?

This is where most mistakes happen.

- **Goods:** since **15 November 2017**, Notification 66/2017-Central Tax lets registered persons, other than composition dealers, pay GST on goods at the time of the invoice, not on receipt of an advance. So a shop that takes 50% advance against a proforma for furniture or laptops pays GST only when it issues the tax invoice.
- **Services:** GST is still payable when you receive an advance. You issue a **receipt voucher** (Section 31(3)(d) and Rule 50), pay the tax on the advance in that month's return, and adjust it when you raise the final tax invoice.
- **Composition dealers** are outside Notification 66/2017. If you are on the composition scheme and take large advances, check the timing with your CA.

The law asks for a receipt voucher whenever an advance is received, even for goods. Many small shops skip it for goods advances because no tax is due on them; your CA will tell you what your records need.

## When businesses use a proforma invoice

| Situation | Why a proforma is asked for |
| --- | --- |
| **Advance payment** from a customer | The buyer's accounts team needs a document showing what they are paying for |
| **Company or government purchases** | The buyer gets internal approval and raises a purchase order against your proforma |
| **Bank loan for machinery or equipment** | Banks sanction loans, including Mudra and subsidy-linked loans, against a supplier's proforma or quotation, and often pay the supplier directly |
| **Government subsidy schemes** | Project reports and subsidy claims list equipment at the prices in suppliers' proformas |
| **Exports** | The foreign buyer uses it to arrange payment, open a letter of credit or apply for an import licence |
| **Imports into India** | Your bank asks for the overseas supplier's proforma before sending an advance remittance |

## What to put on a proforma invoice

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Heading **"PROFORMA INVOICE"** | Make it prominent. Never write "Tax Invoice" on it. |
| Seller's name, address, GSTIN | As registered |
| Proforma number and date | A **separate series**, e.g. PI/26-27/014, not your tax invoice series |
| Buyer's name, address and GSTIN (if any), state | State decides CGST + SGST or IGST |
| Item description, HSN or SAC code | Same descriptions you will use on the final invoice |
| Quantity, unit, rate, amount | Rates excluding GST |
| Estimated GST, rate-wise | CGST + SGST for the same state, IGST for another state |
| Estimated total | In figures (and words if the buyer's bank needs it) |
| **Validity** | e.g. "Valid for 30 days". Banks and subsidy offices check this. |
| Delivery period and place | e.g. "Within 10 days of advance, at buyer's premises" |
| Payment terms | Advance percentage, balance terms, bank details or UPI |
| Line stating it is not a tax invoice | e.g. "This is a proforma invoice, not a tax invoice. A tax invoice will be issued on supply." |
| Signature | Authorised signatory |

For an export proforma, also add the currency, Incoterm (for example FOB Nhava Sheva or CIF Dubai), port of loading and destination, country of origin, packing and weights, HS code, and the bank details the buyer must pay into.

## Sample proforma invoice (GST shown as an estimate)

A computer shop in Pune quoting a new cyber café in the same city. The café owner needs the proforma for a bank loan. Laptops, printers and UPS units are at **18%**.

**SAI COMPUTERS & PERIPHERALS**
12, Laxmi Road, Pune, Maharashtra · GSTIN: 27ABCDE1234F1Z5

**PROFORMA INVOICE** · No. **PI/26-27/014** · Date: 11-11-2026 · **Valid until: 10-12-2026**
To: Om Cyber Café, Kothrud, Pune, Maharashtra · Buyer GSTIN: (if registered)

| # | Item | HSN | Qty | Rate (₹) | Amount (₹) | GST |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Laptop, 14-inch, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD | 8471 | 5 | 42,000.00 | 2,10,000.00 | 18% |
| 2 | Multifunction laser printer | 8443 | 1 | 18,500.00 | 18,500.00 | 18% |
| 3 | UPS 1 kVA | 8504 | 2 | 6,250.00 | 12,500.00 | 18% |
| | **Subtotal** | | | | **2,41,000.00** | |

| Estimate | Amount (₹) |
| --- | --- |
| Taxable value | 2,41,000.00 |
| Estimated CGST 9% | 21,690.00 |
| Estimated SGST 9% | 21,690.00 |
| **Estimated total** | **2,84,380.00** |

**Terms:**
1. This is a proforma invoice, not a tax invoice. A GST tax invoice will be issued at the time of delivery.
2. Prices valid until 10-12-2026. GST will be charged at the rate in force on the date of the tax invoice.
3. Payment: 50% advance (₹1,42,190) with order, balance before delivery. Payment by the buyer's bank directly to our account is accepted.
4. Delivery within 7 working days of advance, at the buyer's premises.
5. Warranty as per manufacturer.

Bank: [account name, account number, IFSC] · For Sai Computers & Peripherals (signature)

**Check it yourself.** ₹42,000 × 5 = ₹2,10,000; ₹6,250 × 2 = ₹12,500; ₹2,10,000 + ₹18,500 + ₹12,500 = ₹2,41,000. 9% of ₹2,41,000 is ₹21,690, so CGST and SGST are ₹21,690 each, ₹43,380 in all (18% of ₹2,41,000). The total is ₹2,41,000 + ₹43,380 = **₹2,84,380**, and half of it is ₹1,42,190. If the café were in another state, the same ₹43,380 would appear as IGST at 18%.

Because these are goods and the shop is a regular taxpayer, the ₹1,42,190 advance attracts no GST when received. GST of ₹43,380 is paid when the tax invoice is issued on delivery.

## Proforma invoice vs quotation vs tax invoice

| | Quotation | Proforma invoice | Tax invoice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stage | Buyer is comparing prices | Buyer has more or less decided | Supply has happened |
| Purpose | Offer a price | Confirm terms, get advance, loan or approval | Charge GST and demand payment |
| Format | Price list with terms | Looks like an invoice | Must follow Rule 46 |
| Legal status under GST | None | None | Required by Section 31 |
| GST liability | No | No (but see advances above) | Yes |
| Reported in GSTR-1 | No | No | Yes |
| Buyer can claim ITC | No | No | Yes, once it appears in GSTR-2B |
| Numbering | Quotation series | Proforma series | Tax invoice series, consecutive and unique per financial year |
| Can change? | Yes | Yes | Only through a credit or debit note |

A quotation answers "how much would it cost?", a proforma answers "this is what I will bill you if you go ahead", and the tax invoice answers "this is what you owe". ## How to convert a proforma into a tax invoice

1. **Wait for the supply.** For goods, the tax invoice is issued at or before removal or delivery. For services, within 30 days of providing the service.
2. **Start a fresh tax invoice** from your regular series. Do not reuse the proforma number or "rename" the proforma.
3. **Update anything that changed:** quantities actually supplied, final prices, and the GST rate in force on the invoice date.
4. **Account for the advance.** Show the advance received and the balance due. For services, adjust the GST already paid on the advance.
5. **Mention the proforma number** in the notes ("Against PI/26-27/014") so both sides can match them.
6. **Report the tax invoice** in GSTR-1 for that month or quarter, and pay the tax through GSTR-3B. Our guide on [how to file GSTR-1](https://billzap.app/blog/how-to-file-gstr-1) covers the return.
7. **Mark the proforma as converted** in your own register, so it is not billed twice.

If goods leave before the invoice is ready, for example for approval or a demo, send them with a [delivery challan](https://billzap.app/blog/delivery-challan-format), not the proforma.

## Common proforma mistakes

- **Using the tax invoice series** for proformas, which leaves gaps or duplicates in your real invoice numbers.
- **Never issuing the tax invoice.** The buyer loses credit and you under-report sales.
- **Reporting proformas in GSTR-1**, which overstates your sales and tax.
- **No validity date.** Banks and subsidy offices often reject an expired or undated proforma.
- **Forgetting GST on service advances.** A consultant or contractor who takes an advance against a proforma owes GST on it that month.
- **A buyer claiming credit on a proforma.** Credit is available only on the tax invoice. Our guide to [input tax credit](https://billzap.app/blog/input-tax-credit-gst) has the conditions.

## FAQ

### What is a proforma invoice in simple words?

A preliminary bill sent before a sale, showing what the seller will supply, at what price, with what taxes and terms. It helps the buyer arrange payment, a loan or an approval. It is not a demand for payment and not a tax invoice.

### Is GST payable on a proforma invoice?

No. Sending a proforma does not create any GST liability. GST becomes payable on the actual supply, through the tax invoice. If you receive an advance for services, GST is payable on that advance, but that comes from the advance, not the proforma.

### Can I claim input tax credit on a proforma invoice?

No. Input tax credit needs a tax invoice or other prescribed document, and the supplier must report it so that it appears in your GSTR-2B. Ask the supplier for the tax invoice when the goods or services are delivered.

### What is the difference between a proforma invoice and a tax invoice?

A proforma comes before supply, has no legal status under GST, can change and is not reported in returns. A tax invoice is issued on supply, must follow Rule 46, carries the GST liability, is reported in GSTR-1 and lets the buyer claim credit.

### Is a proforma invoice the same as a quotation?

Close, but not the same. A quotation offers a price, often to a buyer comparing several sellers. A proforma is more formal, set out like an invoice, and usually sent once the buyer has decided, to arrange an advance, a loan or an order.

### Do banks accept a proforma invoice for a loan?

Yes, banks commonly ask for a supplier's proforma or quotation for machinery and equipment loans. Make sure it has your GSTIN, full item details with HSN codes, GST, a validity date and your bank details, since the bank may pay you directly.

### Can I generate an e-way bill on a proforma invoice?

Goods should not travel on a proforma. Generate the e-way bill against the tax invoice, bill of supply or delivery challan that accompanies the goods. Our guide to [e-way bill rules](https://billzap.app/blog/e-way-bill-rules) covers when one is needed.

## The bottom line

A proforma invoice is a formal promise of what you will bill, not a bill. Give it its own number series, a clear "Proforma Invoice" heading, an estimated GST line and a validity date, and use it for advances, approvals, loans and export payments. When the goods or service actually go out, issue a proper tax invoice, account for any advance, and report only that invoice in your returns.
