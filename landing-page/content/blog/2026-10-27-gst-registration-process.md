Ask around your market how to get a GST number and most people will give you an agent's phone number. The agent will ask for a fee, your PAN, your Aadhaar and your electricity bill, and two weeks later you get a GSTIN. What they do not tell you is that the government charges **nothing** for GST registration, and that the form takes about an hour on the GST portal if your documents are ready.

The process has also changed since most guides were written. Aadhaar authentication now decides how fast you are approved, many applicants are called to a **GST Suvidha Kendra** for a biometric check and a photo, and since 1 November 2025 small businesses can opt for approval in **three working days**. This guide walks through the whole GST registration process as it works today, from deciding whether you need it to issuing your first tax invoice.

## Quick answer

The GST registration process is free and fully online at **gst.gov.in** (Services → Registration → New Registration).

1. **Part A of Form GST REG-01:** enter your PAN, mobile number, email and state. Verify the OTPs and you get a **TRN** (Temporary Reference Number), valid for 15 days.
2. **Part B:** log in with the TRN and fill in business details, owner details, place of business, the goods you sell and your bank account. Upload documents.
3. **Aadhaar authentication:** opt for it. If the system flags your application, you book a slot at a **GST Suvidha Kendra** for biometric and document verification.
4. **Submit** with EVC (OTP) or DSC. You get an **ARN** to track the application.
5. **Approval:** usually within **7 working days**, up to **30 days** if a physical verification of your shop is ordered, and within **3 working days** under the Rule 14A option for small B2B sellers. The certificate (REG-06) and **GSTIN** appear on the portal.

## Do you need GST registration at all?

Before you register, check whether you must. For most shops, registration becomes compulsory when aggregate turnover in a financial year crosses:

| You supply | Limit in most states | Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goods only, within your state | ₹40 lakh | ₹20 lakh |
| Services, or goods and services | ₹20 lakh | ₹10 lakh |

Some businesses must register whatever their turnover, for example anyone making inter-state sales of goods, or selling through an e-commerce operator that collects tax at source. Our guide to the [GST registration limit](https://billzap.app/blog/gst-registration-limit) covers these cases in detail.

### Voluntary registration: should a small shop register early?

You can register below the limit. It is worth it if:

- Your customers are **businesses** who want a tax invoice so they can claim input tax credit.
- You buy a lot of taxed stock and want to **claim the GST you pay** on purchases.
- A distributor, wholesaler or online marketplace insists on a GSTIN before dealing with you.

It is usually not worth it if almost all your customers are walk-in households. Once registered, you must file returns every month or quarter **even in a month with zero sales**, and late fees apply if you miss them. If you want registration with lighter paperwork, look at the [GST composition scheme](https://billzap.app/blog/gst-composition-scheme), which you can choose in the same application.

## Documents required for GST registration

Have scans ready before you start. The size limits are tight: most documents, including photographs and electricity bills, must be a **PDF or JPEG under 100 KB**, and a rent agreement up to **1 MB**. Compress your scans before you begin.

| Document | For a proprietor (most shops) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PAN | Your personal PAN | The business name on the form must match the PAN record |
| Aadhaar | Your Aadhaar, linked to your mobile | Needed for Aadhaar authentication |
| Photograph | Passport-size photo of the owner | JPEG, under 100 KB |
| Proof of business address | Owned: latest property tax receipt, municipal khata or electricity bill | The address on the document must match the address you type |
| | Rented: rent or lease agreement **plus** an electricity bill or property tax receipt of the premises | An unsigned or unstamped rent agreement is a common reason for queries |
| | Family-owned or no rent: **consent letter** from the owner plus the owner's ID and an electricity bill | Write the consent letter on plain paper, signed by the owner |
| Bank proof | Cancelled cheque, first page of passbook or a bank statement showing your name and account number | Can be added after registration (see below) |
| Mobile and email | Your own, not the agent's | Every OTP and notice goes here |

A partnership firm or company also needs the partnership deed or certificate of incorporation, the PAN of the firm or company, details of every partner or director, and an authorisation letter or board resolution for the authorised signatory.

## The GST registration process, step by step

### Step 1: Part A of Form REG-01 (5 minutes)

Go to **gst.gov.in → Services → Registration → New Registration**.

- Select **Taxpayer** as "I am a", then your **state and district**.
- Enter the **legal name of the business exactly as on PAN**, the PAN, your email and mobile number.
- Verify the two OTPs.

You receive a **TRN** by SMS and email. It is valid for **15 days**: Part B must be completed and submitted within that time, or you start again.

### Step 2: Part B, the main application (40 to 60 minutes)

Log in again choosing **Temporary Reference Number (TRN)** and open the draft. Part B has about ten tabs. The ones that matter most for a shop:

- **Business Details:** trade name (the name on your signboard), constitution (Proprietorship for most shops), the date you started or will start, and whether you want the **composition scheme**.
- **Promoter / Partners:** your details, photo and Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
- **Authorised Signatory:** usually you again.
- **Principal Place of Business:** the shop address, nature of possession (own, rented, consented) and the address proof. Use the pin on the map carefully: the portal geo-tags this address.
- **Goods and Services:** the top five goods (by HSN code) or services you deal in. Not sure of the codes? Our guide on [what an HSN code is](https://billzap.app/blog/what-is-hsn-code) explains how to find yours.
- **Bank Accounts:** you can skip this now. Under Rule 10A, bank details must be added within **30 days** of registration or before you file your first GSTR-1, whichever comes first.
- **Aadhaar Authentication:** choose **Yes**. This one choice affects your timeline more than anything else on the form.

### Step 3: Aadhaar authentication and the Suvidha Kendra visit

When you opt for Aadhaar authentication, an authentication link is sent to the mobile and email of the promoter and authorised signatory. Verify it with the Aadhaar OTP.

Since 2025 the GST system also runs a **risk check** on every application. If yours is flagged, or you did not opt for Aadhaar authentication, you get an email asking you to **book an appointment at a GST Suvidha Kendra (GSK)**. There you give a **biometric Aadhaar authentication**, have your photo taken and show the **original documents** you uploaded. Bring the originals of everything in the table above. Since February 2025 you can do this at a GSK in your home state even if the business is registered in another state.

### Step 4: Submit and note the ARN

On the Verification tab, tick the declaration and submit with **EVC** (an OTP to the authorised signatory's mobile) for a proprietor, or a **DSC** for a company or LLP. You get an **ARN** (Application Reference Number) by SMS and email. Track the status under **Services → Registration → Track Application Status**.

### Step 5: Approval, queries or verification

| Situation | When you get a decision |
| --- | --- |
| **Rule 14A option** (you opted in and your output tax on B2B sales is ₹2.5 lakh a month or less), with successful Aadhaar authentication | Within **3 working days**, granted electronically |
| Normal application, Aadhaar authenticated, nothing flagged | Within **7 working days** |
| Physical verification of the shop ordered, or Aadhaar authentication not done | Within **30 days** |

If the officer has a doubt, you get a notice in **Form REG-03**. Reply in **Form REG-04** within **7 working days**, with corrected documents or an explanation. If the reply is not satisfactory, the application is rejected in **REG-05** and you have to apply again.

Once approved, your **GSTIN** and **registration certificate (REG-06)** are available under **Services → User Services → View/Download Certificates**. Your login username and a temporary password arrive by email.

## GST registration fees

The government fee for GST registration is **zero**. There is no fee for the application, the certificate or any amendment. Anyone charging you is charging for their time, which is fine if you want help, but you should know the work is free to do yourself. Never pay anyone who claims to be "from the GST department" for a registration.

## Common reasons a GST application gets a query or is rejected

From the documents most often questioned, in rough order:

1. **Name mismatch with PAN.** The legal name in Part A must match the PAN record letter for letter.
2. **Address proof does not match the typed address.** Shop number, floor, building name and pin code must agree with the electricity bill or rent agreement.
3. **Rent agreement problems:** unsigned, unstamped, expired, or with no utility bill attached.
4. **Electricity bill in someone else's name without a consent letter.**
5. **Missing or blurred photographs** of the owner, or a photo of the shop that does not show the signboard.
6. **Not replying to REG-03 within 7 working days.** A late reply often means rejection, not a second chance.
7. **Skipping Aadhaar authentication**, which turns a 7-day wait into 30 days and a possible site visit.

If you are rejected, read the reason in REG-05, fix that document and apply again. There is no limit on attempts and no fee.

## What to do the day your GSTIN arrives

Registration is the start of your GST life, not the end. In the first week:

- **Display your certificate** at your principal place of business and **paint or print your GSTIN on the signboard** at the entrance. Rule 18 requires both.
- **Add your bank account** on the portal if you skipped it (within 30 days, or before your first GSTR-1).
- **Change your bills.** From the effective date, every sale needs a proper document: a **tax invoice** with your GSTIN, HSN codes and the CGST and SGST (or IGST) split if you charge tax, or a **bill of supply** if you chose composition. Our [GST invoice format](https://billzap.app/blog/gst-invoice-format) guide shows exactly what must be on it.
- **Start a new bill number series** for the financial year, up to 16 characters, with no gaps.
- **Diary your return dates.** A regular taxpayer files [GSTR-1](https://billzap.app/blog/how-to-file-gstr-1) (sales details) by the 11th and [GSTR-3B](https://billzap.app/blog/gstr-3b) (summary and tax payment) by the 20th of the next month, or quarterly under the QRMP scheme if turnover is up to ₹5 crore. A composition dealer pays quarterly through CMP-08 and files GSTR-4 once a year.
- **File even when sales are zero.** A nil return is quick, but a missed one still attracts a late fee.

## FAQ

### How can I get a GST number online for free?

Apply on gst.gov.in under Services → Registration → New Registration. Fill Part A to get a TRN, complete Part B, opt for Aadhaar authentication and submit with an OTP. There is no government fee at any step.

### How many days does GST registration take?

Usually 7 working days after submission. It can take up to 30 days if the officer orders a physical verification of your premises or you did not complete Aadhaar authentication. Small businesses that opt in under Rule 14A, with B2B output tax of ₹2.5 lakh a month or less, are granted registration within 3 working days after Aadhaar authentication.

### What documents are required for GST registration for a shop?

For a proprietor: PAN, Aadhaar, a passport-size photograph, proof of the shop address (electricity bill, property tax receipt or rent agreement with a utility bill, or a consent letter if the premises belong to a family member) and bank proof such as a cancelled cheque. Bank details can be added up to 30 days after registration.

### Can I get GST registration without a shop, from home?

Yes. Your home can be your principal place of business. Use the electricity bill or property tax receipt of the house, and a consent letter if it is in a family member's name.

### Why was I asked to visit a GST Suvidha Kendra?

The GST system flagged your application on its risk parameters, or you did not opt for Aadhaar authentication. You book an appointment, give a biometric Aadhaar authentication, have your photo taken and show the original documents. It is a routine check, not an accusation.

### Can I register for GST with a turnover below ₹40 lakh?

Yes, that is voluntary registration. It helps if you sell to businesses or want to claim input tax credit, but you must file returns every month or quarter from then on, even with no sales.

### What is the difference between TRN, ARN and GSTIN?

The TRN is a temporary number from Part A that lets you finish the form within 15 days. The ARN is the acknowledgement you get after submitting, used to track the application. The GSTIN is your final 15-character GST number, issued on approval.

## The bottom line

The GST registration process is free, online and within reach of any shop owner with a PAN, an Aadhaar-linked mobile and a clean address proof. Opt for Aadhaar authentication, make sure every name and address matches its document, answer any query within seven working days, and you will usually have your GSTIN within a week. Then spend that first week on the part that lasts: correct bills, a clean number series and returns filed on time.
