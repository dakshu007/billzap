Search "ONDC seller registration" and you will find a lot of pages promising that ONDC lets a kirana compete with big e-commerce for a commission of "just 3%". The reality is more mixed. There is no registration form on ONDC's own website for sellers. You join through a **seller app**, each app sets its own fees, and the orders you get depend on how many shoppers in your area use an ONDC buyer app.

This guide walks through how ONDC actually works, the documents and steps, what an order really costs you, the GST rules (including when you can sell without a GSTIN), delivery, and the honest numbers on where ONDC stands in 2026.

## Quick answer

- **ONDC is a network, not an app.** You register through a **seller app** (a Seller Network Participant), which lists your products on every ONDC buyer app.
- **Documents:** PAN, GSTIN or a GST enrolment number, bank account with IFSC, shop address, and a product catalogue with photos and prices. Food sellers also need FSSAI.
- **Steps:** pick a seller app for your category and city, sign up, finish KYC, add your catalogue, set delivery radius and hours, go live.
- **Fees:** ONDC itself does not charge the seller a sign-up fee. You pay the **seller app** (commission or subscription), the **buyer app's** fee, delivery charges and payment charges. These vary by app.
- **GST:** a GSTIN is not always needed. If you sell **goods only within your state** and your turnover is under the registration limit, you can sell with a **GST enrolment number**, if your seller app supports it. Inter-state selling needs a GSTIN.
- **TCS:** the seller app collects **0.5% GST TCS** (0.25% CGST + 0.25% SGST, or 0.5% IGST) on your net taxable sales. You can claim it back.
- **Reality check:** ONDC had over 5 lakh sellers and nearly 90 lakh monthly transactions by June 2026 (PIB). Orders for a single kirana are usually modest; treat it as an extra channel, not a replacement for walk-in sales.

## What ONDC is, and how an order flows

The Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC) is a not-for-profit company backed by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT). It runs a set of common rules that let different apps talk to each other, much as UPI lets any bank app pay any other.

Four parties are involved in every order:

| Who | Role | Examples of what they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buyer app** | Where the customer searches and pays | Shows your products, takes payment, handles the customer side |
| **Seller app** | Where you list and manage your shop | Catalogue, orders, invoices, settlement to your bank, TCS |
| **Logistics provider** | Picks up and delivers, if you do not deliver yourself | Hyperlocal riders or courier |
| **You, the seller** | Pack the order and hand it over | Accept the order, keep stock right, deliver on time |

A customer using a buyer app searches "atta near me", sees your shop alongside others, places the order and pays. Your seller app shows the order, you accept and pack it, a rider (yours or the network's) delivers it, and the seller app settles the money to your bank after deducting fees and taxes.

The point of ONDC: your one listing appears on **every** buyer app on the network, not only one marketplace, and you can move to another seller app without losing access to buyers.

## Do you need GST registration to sell on ONDC?

Not always. Since **1 October 2023**, the GST law allows small sellers to sell goods through e-commerce platforms without a GSTIN, under conditions (Notification 34/2023-Central Tax). Composition dealers can also sell goods through e-commerce from the same date.

| Your situation | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| Selling goods only within your state, turnover under the limit (₹40 lakh for goods in most states, lower in some) | **GST enrolment number** from the GST portal, instead of a GSTIN |
| Composition dealer selling goods within your state | Your composition GSTIN; no inter-state sales |
| Selling to buyers in other states | **Regular GSTIN** |
| Turnover above the registration limit | **Regular GSTIN** |
| Selling only GST-exempt goods (loose grain, fresh vegetables) | Usually no registration; ask your seller app what it accepts |

Conditions for the enrolment route include: a PAN, no inter-state sales, selling through e-commerce in only one state, and declaring your PAN, address and state on the GST portal to get the enrolment number before you sell. Check your turnover against our guide to the [GST registration limit](https://billzap.app/blog/gst-registration-limit).

**Caution:** the law allows it, but each seller app decides whether its onboarding accepts an enrolment number. Some ONDC seller apps do; ask before you sign up. Our guide to [GST for online sellers](https://billzap.app/blog/gst-for-online-sellers) covers the enrolment route in more detail.

### TCS and TDS on ONDC sales

- **GST TCS:** an e-commerce operator collects tax at source on your net taxable sales (sales minus returns): **0.5%** since 10 July 2024 (Notification 15/2024-Central Tax cut the CGST part from 0.5% to 0.25%). On ONDC, several apps touch one order; CBIC Circular 194/06/2023-GST says the TCS is collected by the **seller-side app** that pays you. It shows up on the GST portal and, once you accept it, moves to your electronic cash ledger, where it can pay your GST.
- **Income-tax TDS:** e-commerce operators also deduct **0.1%** income-tax TDS on the gross amount of sales. This was section 194-O; under the Income-tax Act, 2025, in force from 1 April 2026, it sits in the TDS table of **section 393(1)**. An individual or HUF seller whose gross sales through the operator stay within ₹5 lakh in the year and who has given PAN or Aadhaar is generally exempt. TDS deducted shows in your annual tax statement and is claimed in your income-tax return.

## ONDC seller registration: step by step

1. **Pick a seller app.** ONDC's website lists live seller network participants. Choose one that serves your **category** (grocery, food, fashion) and your **city**.
2. **Download it or sign up online** with your mobile number.
3. **Complete KYC:** PAN, GSTIN or enrolment number, bank details, Aadhaar. Approval can take from a few hours to a few days.
4. **Build your catalogue.** Add each product with a clear photo, name with brand and pack size ("Aashirvaad Atta 5 kg", not "atta"), MRP, selling price and GST rate. Many seller apps offer a ready catalogue for branded groceries, which saves hours.
5. **Set store settings:** opening hours, delivery radius, minimum order, whether you deliver yourself or use network logistics, packing time.
6. **Go live and test** with a small order from a buyer app on a friend's phone.
7. **Run the routine:** accept orders quickly, keep stock and prices updated, and mark items out of stock the moment they run out. Cancellations hurt your ranking on buyer apps.

### How to choose a seller app

ONDC's list changes, so check the current one on ondc.org. Seller apps that have served kiranas and small retailers include Mystore and GoFrugal, and Magicpin is widely used by restaurants. Ask each one:

- What do you charge: commission per order, monthly fee, or both?
- How long until money reaches my bank?
- Do you accept a GST enrolment number instead of a GSTIN?
- Do you arrange delivery, and at what rate per order?
- Can I upload my catalogue in bulk?
- Do you offer help under the MSME TEAM scheme (see below)?

You can list with more than one seller app, but keeping stock and prices in sync across two apps is real work for a one-person shop.

## ONDC commission: what an order actually costs you

There is no single "ONDC commission". Each party prices separately:

| Charge | Who sets it | Typical form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seller app fee | Your seller app | Commission per order, monthly subscription, or promotional zero |
| Buyer app fee | The buyer app the customer used | A percentage, usually settled through your seller app |
| Delivery | Logistics provider | Per order, by distance; nil if you deliver |
| Payment charges | Payment gateway | Sometimes absorbed by the app |
| Network fee | ONDC | Announced as ₹1.50 per order above ₹250, charged to seller apps; start dates were deferred more than once, and apps may pass it on |

**Worked example (illustrative rates, not any app's real price list):** a ₹500 grocery order, items at 5% GST (taxable value ₹476.19, GST ₹23.81). Assume a 3% buyer app fee, 2% seller app fee and ₹40 delivery.

| Item | Working | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order value | | ₹500.00 |
| Buyer app fee | ₹500 × 3% | −₹15.00 |
| Seller app fee | ₹500 × 2% | −₹10.00 |
| Delivery | | −₹40.00 |
| GST TCS (recoverable) | ₹476.19 × 0.5% | −₹2.38 |
| Income-tax TDS (recoverable) | ₹500 × 0.1% | −₹0.50 |
| **Credited to your bank** | | **₹432.12** |

The TCS and TDS come back as credits, so the real cost is ₹65, or **13% of the order**. If your margin on groceries is 10 to 15%, delivery decides whether the order is worth taking. Deliver yourself within a short radius, or set a minimum order value, and the cost falls to around 5%. Ask your seller app for its actual rate sheet and run your own numbers before going live.

## Delivery: do it yourself or use the network

- **Self-delivery** suits a kirana with a delivery boy and customers within 2 to 3 km. Cheapest per order, but you carry the timing risk.
- **Network logistics** (hyperlocal riders arranged through your seller app) works for occasional orders or longer distances. You pay per order.
- **Courier** for non-perishables going further. India Post also joined ONDC's logistics side, delivering its first ONDC order in January 2026.

Whatever you choose, set realistic packing and delivery times. Late and cancelled orders push you down the buyer apps' listings.

## The state of ONDC in 2026

- **Size:** by June 2026, ONDC had over **20 crore buyers, 5 lakh sellers**, presence in **1,000 cities** and nearly **90 lakh monthly transactions** (PIB, June 2026).
- **Not straight-line growth:** monthly orders touched a record of about **1.2 crore in July 2024** (Economic Times), when buyer discounts were heavy; the June 2026 figure is lower. Retail growth slowed after ONDC capped incentives in 2025 (Business Standard, March 2025). Without big discounts, shoppers have less reason to switch from the apps they already use.
- **Government support:** the MSME ministry's **TEAM scheme** (Trade Enablement and Marketing), with ₹277.35 crore for 2024-27, aims to bring 5 lakh micro and small enterprises onto ONDC, half of them women-owned. It funds help through seller apps for cataloguing, account management, logistics and packaging. Ask your seller app whether you qualify.

## FAQ

### Is ONDC seller registration free?

ONDC does not charge sellers to sign up. Most seller apps let you register free, then charge a commission per order or a monthly fee. Buyer app fees and delivery are extra.

### Can I sell on ONDC without GST?

Yes, if you sell goods only within your state, your turnover is under the GST limit, and you have a GST enrolment number from the GST portal, provided your seller app accepts it. Sellers of only GST-exempt goods may not need registration at all.

### Which is the best ONDC seller app for a kirana?

It depends on your city and category. Check the current list on ondc.org and compare fees, settlement time, delivery support and whether they help with catalogue setup. Mystore and GoFrugal are among those that have served small retailers.

### How much commission does ONDC charge?

ONDC itself does not take a sales commission. The seller app, buyer app and delivery partner each charge separately, and the total depends on the apps. Work out the cost on a sample order before you go live.

### Who collects TCS on ONDC orders?

The seller-side app that pays you collects GST TCS at 0.5% of net taxable sales, as clarified in CBIC Circular 194/06/2023-GST. You claim it from your electronic cash ledger after accepting it on the GST portal.

### Do I need FSSAI to sell groceries on ONDC?

Yes, for any food items, including packaged foods. Seller apps ask for the FSSAI number during onboarding for food categories.

## The bottom line

ONDC seller registration is simple: pick a seller app, finish KYC with PAN, GSTIN or enrolment number and bank details, add your catalogue and set delivery. The harder part is the economics. Work out what an order costs you after app fees and delivery, keep stock accurate so you do not cancel orders, and treat ONDC as one more channel next to your counter, WhatsApp and Google Maps.
