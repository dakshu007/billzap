If you sell a service, every line on your GST invoice needs a **SAC code** and the right rate. Most SAC lists online are copied from 2017: they still show 12% and 28% slabs for services, give gyms and salons 18%, and list hotel rooms at 12%. All of that changed on **22 September 2025**, when GST rates were reset.

This guide gives you the 60-odd SAC codes that shops and small service businesses actually bill, with the rate that applies now, grouped by trade. It also explains how the code is built, how many digits your bill must show, how SAC differs from HSN, and where to check a code yourself.

## Quick answer

- **SAC** (Services Accounting Code) classifies **services** under GST. Every SAC code starts with **99**, because services sit in chapter 99 of India's tariff.
- **Structure:** 99 + two digits = **heading** (9987: repair services), one more = **group** (99871), one more = the full **6-digit code** (998714: repair of motor vehicles).
- **Digits on your invoice:** turnover up to **₹5 crore** last year: at least **4 digits** on B2B invoices (optional on B2C). Above ₹5 crore: **6 digits** on all invoices (Notification 78/2020-Central Tax).
- **Most services are 18%.** The main exceptions since 22 September 2025: restaurants, salons, barbers, gyms and yoga, hotel rooms up to ₹7,500 a night and cabs are **5% without input tax credit**; tailoring and some job work are **5%**; individual life and health insurance is **exempt**.
- **Check a code** with the HSN/SAC search on the GST portal or the services rate page on the CBIC website.

## How a SAC code is built

The SAC list comes from the "Scheme of Classification of Services" annexed to Notification 11/2017-Central Tax (Rate), the notification that also sets the rate for each service. It works like a tree:

| Level | Digits | Example | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chapter | 2 | 99 | All services |
| Heading | 4 | 9963 | Accommodation, food and beverage services |
| Group | 5 | 99633 | Food, edible preparations and beverage serving services |
| Service code | 6 | 996331 | Services by restaurants, cafes and similar eating places, including takeaway and door delivery |

The headings, grouped the way the scheme groups them:

| Headings | What they cover |
| --- | --- |
| 9954 | Construction services |
| 9961 to 9969 | Trade on commission, accommodation and food, passenger and goods transport, courier, utilities |
| 9971 to 9973 | Financial services, real estate and renting, leasing of goods |
| 9981 to 9989 | Professional and business services: R&D, legal and accounting, consultancy and IT, telecom, support services, repairs, job work, printing |
| 9991 to 9999 | Community and personal services: education, health, recreation, laundry, beauty and others |

One point that confuses shopkeepers: **9961 and 9962 ("wholesale and retail trade services") are not for shops selling goods.** They cover trading done on commission or for a fee. If you sell biscuits or phones, those are goods and take HSN codes. You use SAC only for the services you sell.

## How many SAC digits must your invoice show?

The rule is the same one that applies to HSN codes for goods, set by **Notification 78/2020-Central Tax** (in force since 1 April 2021). It looks at your **aggregate turnover in the previous financial year**:

| Previous-year turnover | B2B invoices | B2C invoices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to ₹5 crore | At least 4 digits (e.g. 9987) | Optional |
| Above ₹5 crore | At least 6 digits (e.g. 998714) | At least 6 digits |

Showing all 6 digits is always allowed and is the better habit: 9963 alone does not say whether you sold a room or a meal. The same codes go into **Table 12 of GSTR-1**, the HSN/SAC-wise summary, where services are reported with the unit "NA" (not applicable) instead of a quantity. Our guide on [how to file GSTR-1](https://billzap.app/blog/how-to-file-gstr-1) shows that table.

## SAC vs HSN: the difference

| | HSN | SAC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classifies | Goods | Services |
| Origin | World Customs Organization's Harmonized System | Indian scheme, carried from service tax into GST |
| First digits | 01 to 98 | Always 99 |
| Length used in India | 4, 6 or 8 digits | 4, 5 or 6 digits |
| Example | 8517: mobile phones | 998716: mobile phone repair |

A business that sells both uses both on the same bill. For the goods side, our guide on [what an HSN code is](https://billzap.app/blog/what-is-hsn-code) covers the 8-digit structure.

## Worked example: a bill with SAC and HSN lines

An appliance repair shop in Indore, registered under GST, services a customer's split AC and replaces a capacitor, billing them separately.

| Line | Code | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AC servicing and repair (labour) | SAC 998715 | ₹1,200.00 |
| Capacitor (part) | HSN 8532 | ₹350.00 |
| Taxable value | | ₹1,550.00 |
| CGST 9% | | ₹139.50 |
| SGST 9% | | ₹139.50 |
| **Total** | | **₹1,829.00** |

Check: 18% of ₹1,550 is ₹279, split equally as ₹139.50 + ₹139.50, and ₹1,550 + ₹279 = ₹1,829. Both lines are 18% here, but a 5% service sold with an 18% product would not match, which is why each line needs its own code and rate. ## Where to look up a SAC code officially

1. **GST portal HSN/SAC search.** On the GST portal, **Services > User Services > Search HSN Code** (no login needed) lets you search by keyword or code and choose services. It shows the description against each code.
2. **CBIC's "GST Goods and Services Rates" page.** The services section lists the rate notifications by heading. The rate itself lives in Notification 11/2017-Central Tax (Rate) as amended, most recently for rates by Notification 15/2025.
3. **Exemptions** are in a separate notification, 12/2017-Central Tax (Rate), which is why exempt services such as healthcare or road transport by a non-GTA do not appear with a rate in the main list.
4. **Your CA**, for anything that could fall under two headings, such as a service bundled with goods.

## Common SAC mistakes

- **Using an HSN code for a service**, or a SAC code for goods. Services always start with 99.
- **Using 9962 for a retail shop.** Selling goods is not a "retail trade service".
- **Copying an old rate list.** Salon at 18%, hotel rooms at 12% and GTA at 12% with ITC are all out of date since 22 September 2025. See our [GST rates list](https://billzap.app/blog/gst-rates-list) for the goods side.
- **Claiming ITC on a "no ITC" service.** A restaurant, salon or gym at 5% cannot take credit on its inputs.
- **Forgetting RCM.** Legal fees from an advocate, security services from a non-corporate agency, GTA freight and rent from an unregistered landlord can all put the tax on you, the recipient.

## FAQ

### What is a SAC code in GST?

SAC stands for Services Accounting Code. It is a code beginning with 99 that classifies a service under GST, the way an HSN code classifies goods. It decides the rate and is shown on the tax invoice and in GSTR-1.

### How many digits is a SAC code?

The full code has 6 digits, such as 998714. If your turnover last year was up to ₹5 crore, you must show at least the first 4 digits on B2B invoices; above ₹5 crore, all 6 digits on every invoice.

### What is the GST rate on most services in 2026?

18%. The main exceptions are restaurants, salons, gyms, hotel rooms up to ₹7,500 a night, cabs and tour operators at 5% without input tax credit, tailoring and some job work at 5%, and exempt services such as healthcare, school education and individual life and health insurance.

### What is the SAC code for repair services?

Heading 9987. Vehicle repair is 998714, computer repair 998713, appliance repair 998715 and mobile phone repair 998716. All are 18%.

### What is the SAC code for rent?

997212 for commercial property such as a shop or godown (18%), and 997211 for a residential dwelling (exempt when rented for living).

### What is the difference between SAC and HSN codes?

HSN codes classify goods and come from the international Harmonized System. SAC codes classify services, were made in India and always start with 99. A bill can have both.

### Where can I find the official SAC code list?

On the GST portal's HSN/SAC search and on the CBIC website's services rate page. The complete scheme is annexed to Notification 11/2017-Central Tax (Rate).

## The bottom line

Find your trade above, note the 6-digit code and rate, and check it once on the GST portal. Most services are 18%. Salons, gyms, hotel rooms and cabs changed in September 2025 to 5% without input tax credit, and many lists online have not caught up.
