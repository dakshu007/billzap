If you sell food in India, from a kirana counter, a tea stall, a sweet shop or a home kitchen, you need FSSAI registration. That part has not changed. Almost everything else about it changed on **1 April 2026**, and much of what you read online, and what many agents still tell shop owners, is out of date.

The two changes that matter: **basic registration now covers food businesses with turnover up to ₹1.5 crore**, up from ₹12 lakh, so nearly every small shop needs only the cheapest tier. And registrations and licences issued from that date have **perpetual validity**: there is no renewal. This guide explains which tier your shop needs, how to apply on FoSCoS yourself for ₹100 a year, and the one detail most shops miss, which is printing the FSSAI number on their bills.

## Quick answer

- **Who needs it:** every food business, including kirana and general stores, tea and snack stalls, sweet shops, bakeries, home cooks who sell, and medical stores that sell food items such as health drinks or supplements.
- **Which tier, from 1 April 2026:**
  - **Basic Registration:** annual turnover up to **₹1.5 crore**. Fee **₹100 a year**.
  - **State Licence:** turnover above ₹1.5 crore and up to **₹50 crore**.
  - **Central Licence:** turnover above ₹50 crore (and some activities, such as import, at any size).
- **Validity:** registrations and licences issued on or after 1 April 2026 stay valid **unless suspended, cancelled or surrendered**. No renewal. The annual fee still applies, and you can pay for several years at once.
- **Where:** only on **foscos.fssai.gov.in**, using **Form A** for basic registration.
- **On your bills:** the **14-digit FSSAI number** must be printed on cash memos, bills and invoices.

## What changed on 1 April 2026

FSSAI amended the Licensing and Registration Regulations in March 2026 and set new turnover thresholds from 1 April 2026. FSSAI's own FAQs, issued on 27 March 2026, confirm the new bands:

| Tier | Before 1 April 2026 | From 1 April 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Registration | Turnover up to ₹12 lakh | Turnover up to **₹1.5 crore** |
| State Licence | ₹12 lakh to ₹20 crore | Above ₹1.5 crore up to **₹50 crore** |
| Central Licence | Above ₹20 crore | Above **₹50 crore** |
| Validity | 1 to 5 years, then renew | **Perpetual**, no renewal |

Three practical consequences:

1. **A shop with ₹20 lakh to ₹1.5 crore turnover no longer needs a State licence.** Existing licence holders are being moved to registration through FoSCoS, with no modification fee and the **same number**. Fees already paid for the licence are adjusted against the registration fee.
2. **"FSSAI license renewal" is now mostly a non-issue** for anything issued from 1 April 2026. Older certificates move to the new system as FSSAI completes the migration; check your status on FoSCoS rather than paying anyone to "renew".
3. **Street vendors registered under the Street Vendors Act** are now deemed to be registered under FSSAI. They still have to follow the hygiene rules.

Manufacturers have a second test besides turnover: production capacity. A sweet shop or small bakery that makes its own products can stay on basic registration only within the capacity limits set for its kind of business (around 100 kg or litres a day for most foods, with separate limits for milk and meat). The **Kind of Business** list on FoSCoS shows the exact limit when you apply.

## Which FSSAI registration does your shop need?

| Business | Typical turnover | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kirana or general store | ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore | Basic Registration (₹100 a year) |
| Tea stall, snack counter, pan shop selling packaged food | Under ₹20 lakh | Basic Registration, listed as "Petty retailer of snacks/tea shops" |
| Sweet shop or bakery making its own products | ₹30 lakh to ₹1.5 crore | Basic Registration if within the manufacturing capacity limit; otherwise State Licence |
| Medical store selling health drinks, protein powders, baby food | Under ₹1.5 crore | Basic Registration (FSSAI covers the food items, not the medicines) |
| Home baker or tiffin service | Under ₹20 lakh | Basic Registration |
| Supermarket or wholesale distributor | ₹1.5 crore to ₹50 crore | State Licence |

Turnover here means **food business turnover** for the year. If you are just starting, use your honest estimate for the first year; FoSCoS asks you to declare it.

## How to apply for FSSAI basic registration on FoSCoS

The whole process takes about 30 minutes if your documents are scanned.

### Step 1: Check eligibility

Open **foscos.fssai.gov.in** and choose **Apply for Licence/Registration**. Select your **state**, then your **kind of business** (for example "Retailer" for a kirana store), and answer the turnover question. FoSCoS tells you whether you need Registration, a State Licence or a Central Licence.

### Step 2: Sign up and open Form A

Sign up with your mobile number and email, verify the OTP and log in. Choose **New Registration**, which opens **Form A** (Form B is for licences).

### Step 3: Fill in the details

- **Name and address of the business** exactly as you want it on the certificate
- **Your name, address and contact details**
- **Kind of business and food categories** you deal in: grains and pulses, edible oil, packaged snacks, dairy products, beverages and so on. List everything you sell.
- **Turnover** for the year
- **Number of years** you want to pay for

### Step 4: Upload documents

For basic registration, keep these ready:

| Document | Accepted examples |
| --- | --- |
| Photograph of the owner | Recent passport-size photo |
| Photo ID of the owner | Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID or driving licence |
| Proof of possession of the premises | Rent agreement, electricity bill, property tax receipt or sale deed |
| Food categories list | Selected in the form; some states ask for a short declaration |

A partnership or company adds the partnership deed or incorporation certificate and an authorisation for the person applying.

### Step 5: Pay the fee and submit

Pay **₹100 for each year** you choose, online. You get an **application reference number** by SMS and email; use it to track the status on FoSCoS.

### Step 6: Approval

Under the regulations, the registering authority either grants registration within **7 days** or orders an inspection of your premises first, in which case the decision follows the inspection, within **30 days**. Once approved, download the **registration certificate** from FoSCoS. It carries your **14-digit FSSAI registration number**.

## FSSAI registration fees in 2026

| Tier | Government fee |
| --- | --- |
| Basic Registration | **₹100 a year** |
| State Licence | About ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 a year, depending on the kind of business (the FoSCoS fee page lists each) |
| Central Licence | Higher; see FoSCoS |

With perpetual validity, you can pay for **any number of years at once**, at any time of the year. Keep the fee paid: validity is perpetual, but the annual fee is still due.

### Watch out for agents and lookalike websites

Search "FSSAI registration" and the first results include private websites with official-looking names that charge a "platform fee" or "consultancy fee" many times the ₹100 government fee for the same application. Some are legitimate consultants, some are not. The only official application site is **foscos.fssai.gov.in**, and FSSAI does not charge any processing fee above the government fee. If you do use an agent, insist that the application is filed in **your** name, with **your** mobile number and email, so that every OTP and certificate reaches you.

## The FSSAI number on your bills

This is the requirement most small shops miss. An FSSAI order of 2021 made it **mandatory for all food businesses to print their 14-digit FSSAI licence or registration number on cash receipts, purchase invoices, cash memos and bills**. FSSAI and the Central Consumer Protection Authority have since written to states asking for special drives to enforce it, after complaints from consumers. The only documents exempted are system-generated government documents such as the GST e-way bill.

What this means at your counter:

- **Every bill** you give a customer should show the FSSAI number, whether it is a handwritten cash memo, a printed bill book page or a bill from an app.
- If you also issue a separate challan or delivery note, the number goes on that too.
- **Display your registration certificate** at a prominent place in the shop, where customers can see it.

If your bill book is already printed, add a rubber stamp with the number. If you bill on your phone, put the number in your shop details so it prints automatically. Our guide to [bill format](https://billzap.app/blog/bill-format) shows where it fits in the header, along with your GSTIN.

## Penalties for running without FSSAI registration

The Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 treats food business without the required licence or registration seriously. Under Section 63, carrying on a food business without the required licence can be punished with **imprisonment of up to six months and a fine of up to ₹5 lakh**. In practice, a food safety officer who finds an unregistered shop usually issues an improvement notice and expects you to register at once, but the risk is not worth it for a ₹100 registration.

Separate penalties apply for selling unsafe, misbranded or sub-standard food, whether or not you are registered. Registration is the floor, not the ceiling.

## FAQ

### What is the fee for FSSAI basic registration in 2026?

₹100 a year, paid online on FoSCoS. You can pay for several years at once. There is no separate processing fee from FSSAI.

### Does a kirana store need an FSSAI licence or registration?

Almost always just basic registration. From 1 April 2026, any food business with annual turnover up to ₹1.5 crore needs registration, not a licence. A store with turnover above ₹1.5 crore and up to ₹50 crore needs a State licence.

### Do I need to renew my FSSAI registration?

Not if it was issued on or after 1 April 2026. These have perpetual validity and remain valid unless suspended, cancelled or surrendered. You still pay the annual fee. For older certificates, check your status on FoSCoS as FSSAI migrates them to the new system.

### How long does FSSAI registration take?

The registering authority can grant it within 7 days of a complete application, or order an inspection first, in which case the decision comes within 30 days. Many applications are approved within a few days.

### Is it mandatory to print the FSSAI number on bills?

Yes. FSSAI has required all food businesses to print their 14-digit licence or registration number on cash memos, bills, cash receipts and invoices since 2021, and has asked states to enforce it.

### Does a medical store need FSSAI registration?

Only for the food items it sells, such as health drinks, protein powders, baby food or nutraceuticals. Medicines are licensed under drug laws, but a chemist that sells food products needs FSSAI registration too.

### Can I apply for FSSAI registration without a shop?

Yes. A home kitchen, home bakery or online food seller can register using the home address, with an electricity bill or rent agreement as proof of possession.

## The bottom line

The 2026 changes made FSSAI cheaper and simpler for small shops: one ₹100-a-year registration up to ₹1.5 crore turnover, applied for in half an hour on FoSCoS, and never renewed. Apply in your own name on the official site, display the certificate, and make sure your 14-digit number is on every bill you hand over. That last step is the one most shops still forget.
