## GST compliance depth

This is where Vyapar and myBillBook genuinely lead, and it should decide your choice if it applies to you.

**E-invoicing** (generating an IRN on the government portal) is mandatory for businesses whose aggregate turnover crossed ₹5 crore in any financial year since 2017-18. Vyapar supports it, with IRN and QR generation, and myBillBook includes it on Pro Max. BillZap does not generate e-invoices.

**E-way bills** are needed when you move goods worth more than ₹50,000. Vyapar includes 10 a month on Silver and unlimited on Gold; myBillBook includes unlimited e-way bills on Pro Max. BillZap does not generate e-way bills.

**Tally export** matters if your CA keeps your books in Tally. Vyapar Gold exports to Tally, and myBillBook Pro Max does on request. BillZap gives your CA a month-by-month GSTR-1 summary of taxable value, CGST, SGST and IGST, a profit and loss statement and sales reports, as PDF or CSV, which covers most small shops' monthly filing.

What all three get right is the invoice itself. Each applies GST per item, and each decides CGST plus SGST against IGST from the place of supply. On a ₹3,740 sale at 18%, that is CGST ₹336.60 plus SGST ₹336.60 inside your state, or IGST ₹673.20 to another state. Each also handles the rate structure in force since 22 September 2025, where most goods sit at 5% or 18% and luxury and sin goods at 40%. ## Offline use and where your data lives

These three apps are built on different ideas about where your books should live, and it is worth deciding which one you want.

**myBillBook** keeps your data in its cloud, tied to your account. The upside is real: lose the phone and nothing is lost, and every device sees the same books. The cost is that the app is designed around a connection and an account.

**Vyapar** sits in between. It calls itself offline-first: items, parties and invoices are saved on your phone or desktop, so you can bill with no connection, and paid plans sync the data across devices and share it with staff or your accountant once you are back online.

**BillZap** keeps everything on the phone. There is no server: invoices never leave the device unless you share them yourself, as a PDF you chose to send. The app works identically with the network off, nothing is queued waiting to sync, and there is no account to lose access to. App lock with a PIN or fingerprint protects it, and a backup is a single file you keep and restore on a new phone. The honest cost is the mirror image of myBillBook's benefit: if you never take a backup and lose the phone, the data goes with it. Take the backup.

If more than one person bills, this section decides it.

**BillZap** is one phone. Your CA gets exports, not a login. For a single counter that is simpler and cheaper; for a team it is the wrong tool, and we would rather say so here than have you find out in week two.
